Privacy Policy – Frostane Reels Automation

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Frostane Reels Automation ("the app", Meta App ID 2185791365667203) is a private tool operated by the owner of the Facebook Page Frostane. It publishes the owner's own original short videos (Reels) to that Page through Meta's official Graph API. Contact: frostanegg@gmail.com.

Who uses the app

Only the Page owner. The app has no public sign-up, no other users and no login for anyone else.

Facebook permissions

The app requests only: pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts and business_management (needed because the Page belongs to a business portfolio), plus Meta's default public_profile.

What data the app accesses

  • The owner's own account, once at setup: the owner's Facebook user ID and name, the permissions granted, and the list of Pages the owner manages (used only to select the Frostane Page). The owner's user access token is used for these checks and is not stored.
  • The Frostane Page: its Page ID and name, and an access token issued by Meta for that Page, with token details from Meta's token debugger (type, permissions, expiry dates, and the ID of the user who granted it).
  • Reels already on the Page: video ID, caption, publish date, length and link. To avoid posting the same video twice, the video stream is read once to compute small visual fingerprints. The video itself is not saved.
  • Reels the app publishes: the video ID and publishing status Meta returns.
  • The owner's own local video files and the captions written for them. Captions may be suggested from text inside the video using the computer's built-in text recognition, which runs on the device.

The app does not access, collect or store personal data of followers, viewers or other Facebook users, and it does not read comments, messages, likes or insights.

How the data is used

Only to publish the owner's videos to the owner's Page, check publishing status, confirm the app is posting to the right Page, and prevent duplicate posts. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling.

Where data is stored

On the owner's own computer. The Page access token is kept in Windows Credential Manager. Publishing records, video fingerprints, preview stills used for caption review, and activity logs (with tokens removed) stay in a local database, its backup copies, and local files. The app sends data only to Meta (Facebook), through its official API.

Retention and deletion

Local records are kept until the owner deletes them; the publishing history is kept so the same video is never posted twice. The owner can delete all local data at any time by removing the app's folder and its Windows Credential Manager entry, and can revoke the app's access in Facebook (Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations, or Apps and websites). Removing local data does not delete Reels already published; those are deleted from the Page on Facebook. To request deletion of any data or to ask a question, contact: frostanegg@gmail.com.
